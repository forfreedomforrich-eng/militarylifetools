<p>Military retirement planning can feel complicated because the rules are different depending on when you joined and which system applies to you. A simple explanation helps you understand whether the legacy pension, BRS, or TSP strategy makes more sense for your goals.</p>

<h2>Key questions this guide answers</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Which retirement system applies to me?</li>
  <li>How do Legacy and BRS differ?</li>
  <li>How much does TSP contribution matter?</li>
  <li>What does a retirement calculator estimate?</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>Retirement planning works best when you understand the basic structure first. The calculator gives you a starting point, but the bigger decision is how to combine pension, TSP, and lifestyle expectations over time. Members who joined before January 1, 2018 are typically under the Legacy system (defined benefit only). Members who joined after that date are usually under BRS (Blended Retirement System), which includes a smaller pension plus a 1% automatic TSP match and a 5% match up to your own contribution.</p>
