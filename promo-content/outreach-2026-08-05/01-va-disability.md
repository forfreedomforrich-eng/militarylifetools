**Post title**: VA Disability Guide: Understanding Compensation and Claims
**Tags**: va, disability, military, veterans, benefits, compensation

```html
<p>VA disability compensation is one of the most important benefits for veterans with service-connected conditions. The claims process can feel overwhelming — and the rating system is detailed. That's why many veterans look for a clear, practical explanation before they file a claim or review a decision.</p>

<h2>What this guide covers</h2>
<ul>
  <li>How VA disability ratings work (0% to 100%)</li>
  <li>What "service connection" really means</li>
  <li>How a planning estimate helps before you file</li>
  <li>What to review before filing or appealing a claim</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this matters for veterans</h2>
<p>Most veterans want a practical starting point — not a legal lecture. A clear breakdown helps you understand the basics and decide whether you need a deeper review or a quick planning estimate. The rating you receive affects your monthly compensation, your health care eligibility, and your long-term financial planning.</p>

<h2>Quick breakdown of ratings</h2>
<p>Ratings are assigned in 10% increments. Each rating tier maps to a fixed monthly compensation amount that the VA publishes annually. The rating combines all your service-connected conditions — not a single one — so multiple conditions are added together using VA math.</p>

Compare the estimate with your own records and claim documents before submitting to the VA.</p>
