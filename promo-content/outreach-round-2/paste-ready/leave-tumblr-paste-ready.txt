TITLE: How Much Military Leave Do You Actually Have? Most Service Members Have No Idea

Quick leave-balance explainer for Tumblr — easy to share, easy to reblog, useful for service members who want to know their balance before ETS.

Most service members do not track their military leave balance carefully. That is a problem, because leave is one of the most valuable benefits in the entire military compensation package, and it can disappear quickly if you do not plan ahead.

Here is the basic math:

- Active-duty service members earn 2.5 days of leave per month
- That works out to 30 days per year
- There is no cap on how much you can carry over

So if you have been in for 5 years and have used very little leave, you might have 60, 90, or even more than 100 days sitting on the books.

WHY THE 60-DAY WINDOW MATTERS

When you separate from active duty, you have 60 days to either use or sell back your leave. After that window closes, any remaining leave is lost.

This is why many service members plan terminal leave — taking time off between their last day of work and their official separation date. Terminal leave gives you a few extra weeks to transition, travel, or just decompress before the next chapter.

If you would rather have the cash, you can sell back your leave balance at your basic pay rate on the day of separation. Many service members split the difference and do a little of both.

WHY THE LEAVE YEAR CONFUSES PEOPLE

The military leave year runs from October 1 to September 30, not January to December. This catches a lot of people off guard, especially when they try to count leave by calendar year.

If you are trying to figure out how much leave you should have at a given future date, the easiest way is to use a leave calculator. Enter your current balance, leave used so far this year, and your ETS date, and you will get a quick estimate.

A calculator is not an official record — your LES is. But an estimate can help you avoid the surprise of having less leave than you expected, or the bigger surprise of letting some of it expire.
