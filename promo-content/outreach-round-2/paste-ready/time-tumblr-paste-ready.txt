TITLE: Military Time Isn't Hard — Stop Overthinking It

Quick military time explainer for Tumblr — shareable, reblog-friendly, useful for civilians who keep seeing 24-hour timestamps and don't know how to read them.

If you have ever stared at 1730 on a flight itinerary and thought "what time is that in normal person language?", here is the only rule you need:

Military time runs from 0000 (midnight) to 2359 (one minute before the next midnight).

For AM times, just write the hour as a 2-digit number. So 1:30 AM = 0130, 9:45 AM = 0945.

For PM times, add 12 to the hour. So 1:00 PM = 1300, 5:30 PM = 1730, 11:59 PM = 2359.

The two exceptions:

- 12:00 PM (noon) = 1200 (not 2400)
- 12:00 AM (midnight) = 0000

That is the whole system. Nothing else.

WHY IT EXISTS OUTSIDE THE MILITARY

The 24-hour clock is a global standard. Hospitals use it so patients don't get the wrong medication at the wrong time. Airlines use it because flights cross time zones all day. Emergency services use it because "1:00" without AM/PM is dangerous.

It is not a military quirk. It is a precision tool, and once you know the rule, you will read it everywhere.
