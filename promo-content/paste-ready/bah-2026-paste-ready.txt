HOW BAH WORKS IN 2026:

BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) is a monthly allowance paid to active-duty service members who don't live in government-provided housing on base.

The 4 Factors That Determine Your BAH:
1. Duty Station ZIP Code (MHA) — the single biggest factor
2. Pay Grade (Rank) — officers and senior enlisted receive more
3. Dependency Status — with dependents rates are 10-30% higher
4. Whether You Live On or Off Base — on-base housing forfeits BAH

Q: Do I get BAH if I live in government housing on base?
A: No, you do not receive BAH if you live in on-base government quarters.

Q: Is BAH taxable in 2026?
A: No, BAH is tax-free at the federal level, and in most states it's also exempt from state income tax.
