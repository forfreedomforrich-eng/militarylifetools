BAH Calculator Guide: How Basic Allowance for Housing Works

July 14, 2026 | 10 min read | By Military Life Tools

Basic Allowance for Housing, or BAH, is one of the most important monthly benefits for many active-duty service members. It helps offset housing costs when you are not living in government-provided quarters. Because a housing allowance can change based on rank, duty station, and dependency status, many people search for a BAH calculator before making a housing plan.

What affects BAH?

  • Rank or pay grade (E-1 through O-10)
  • Duty station ZIP code (mapped to a Military Housing Area)
  • Whether you have dependents (with vs. without dependents)
  • Whether you are living in the barracks or off-base

How BAH is calculated in 2026

The Department of Defense sets BAH rates every year using rental market data collected from each Military Housing Area (MHA). For 2026, the average rate increased by approximately 5.2% to keep pace with median rent growth. The formula looks at three things: local median rent for your rank's housing profile, average utilities, and a pay-grade adjustment. The result is a single monthly rate designed to cover roughly 95% of housing costs for service members in that area.

Why rate tables alone are not enough

Most service members find the official BAH rate tables hard to read. The tables list dozens of ZIP codes and rank combinations, and it is easy to misread a row. A calculator turns those tables into a single number based on your inputs, removing the guesswork and saving you from scrolling through pages of data.

Why this matters

A simple estimate helps you understand how much housing support you may receive and how that fits into your monthly budget. It does not replace official rates, but it is useful for planning before a move or relocation. Many families use the estimate to decide whether to rent or buy, where to live, and how much to set aside for utilities.

Frequently asked questions

What is BAH?

BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) is a tax-free monthly allowance paid to active-duty service members who do not live in government-provided housing. It helps offset the cost of off-base rent or mortgage and varies by duty station ZIP code, rank, and dependency status.

How accurate is a BAH calculator?

A BAH calculator provides a close estimate based on published 2026 DoD rate tables, but only your finance office can confirm the exact amount. Use it for planning, not for final budgeting decisions.

Does BAH include utilities?

Yes. BAH is calculated to cover rent plus average utilities (electric, gas, water, sewer). The DoD computes a total housing cost for each Military Housing Area (MHA) and sets the rate to cover approximately 95% of that cost.

How does BAH differ from BAS?

BAH covers housing; BAS (Basic Allowance for Subsistence) covers food. Both are tax-free, but BAS is a flat rate that does not vary by location, while BAH varies by ZIP code and rank.

When is BAH paid each month?

BAH is paid on the 1st and 15th of each month, split into two equal halves, like base pay. It starts the day you report to your new duty station and stops the day you leave.

What if my BAH seems wrong?

If your BAH does not match your expected rate, contact your local finance office (DFAS or your branch equivalent). Common causes include outdated dependency data, incorrect ZIP code, or pending promotion effective dates.
