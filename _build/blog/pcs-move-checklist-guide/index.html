PCS Move Checklist Guide: What to Do Before Your Military Relocation

July 13, 2026 | 8 min read | By Military Life Tools

PCS moves are stressful because the timeline is packed with orders, deadlines, paperwork, and family logistics. A good checklist can help reduce the chaos and make the process feel manageable.

Many service members search for a PCS move checklist because they need something practical and easy to follow. The key is to break the move into phases so you can focus on the most important tasks first.

What a good PCS checklist should include

  • Orders and move dates
  • Housing decisions and lease planning
  • Medical, school, and pet paperwork
  • Shipment and storage planning
  • Travel and temporary lodging logistics

Why planning early matters

Starting early helps you avoid last-minute surprises. If you begin planning 60 to 90 days before the move, you have more time to compare housing options, arrange travel, and confirm paperwork.

How to use the checklist tool

Use the PCS move checklist tool to generate a simple timeline based on your move date and branch. It can help turn a complex relocation into a manageable sequence of milestones.

Bottom line

The best PCS plans are simple, realistic, and started early. A checklist keeps the process organized so you can focus on the move without losing track of the essentials.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start planning my PCS move?

Start planning 10-12 months before your projected move date. The military recommends beginning preliminary research 12 months out, with concrete steps starting around 6 months before. The earlier you start, the better your housing, school, and shipment choices will be.

What is a PCS checklist?

A PCS checklist is a sequenced list of tasks for a military move, including orders, housing, medical records, school enrollment, shipment, and travel. It is the single most useful tool for keeping a complex move on track.

Do I get DLA for a PCS?

Active-duty service members on PCS orders who relocate their dependents are eligible for DLA. The amount is typically one to two months of BAH, depending on whether you have dependents. Use a BAH calculator to estimate your DLA amount.

What is TLA?

TLA (Temporary Lodging Allowance) reimburses service members for lodging and meals when they are at a new duty station but do not yet have permanent housing. OCONUS service members typically receive TLA for up to 60 days; CONUS members in some locations may also qualify.

Can I do a DITY move?

Yes. Service members can choose a Personally Procured Move (PPM), formerly called DITY. You receive the same government payment it would cost a contracted mover, plus an incentive (typically 95% of the estimated cost). You keep any savings if you move for less.

What is the weight allowance for a PCS move?

Weight allowance depends on rank and family status. For example, an E-5 with dependents is authorized 18,000 pounds (HHG) plus 2,000 pounds for professional gear. Officers and senior NCOs have higher allowances. Check the Defense Personal Property Program (DPS) for your specific allowance.
