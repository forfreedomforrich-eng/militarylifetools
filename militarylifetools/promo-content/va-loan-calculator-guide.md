As a service member, one of the best financial benefits you have access to is the **VA loan program**. Backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs, VA loans offer some of the most competitive mortgage terms available in today's housing market.

But here's the challenge: **how much house can you actually afford?**

That's exactly why we built our free **VA Loan Calculator** — to help military members and veterans figure out their borrowing power before starting their home search.

### What is a VA Loan?

A VA loan is a mortgage program specifically for:
- Active duty service members
- Veterans who served at least 90 consecutive days during wartime
- National Guard and Reserve members
- Eligible surviving spouses

The best part? **No down payment required.**

### Why Use a VA Loan Calculator?

Before you start viewing homes, you need to know your budget. Our calculator takes into account:
- Your monthly income

- Current VA loan interest rates
- Funding fees and taxes

This gives you a realistic picture of what you can afford — saving you time and helping you make smarter financial decisions.

### VA Loan Limits in 2026

For 2026, VA loan limits vary by county. In high-cost areas, you may be able to borrow significantly more than the standard conforming loan limits.

Our calculator uses current 2026 VA loan limits to give you the most accurate estimate possible.

### Tips for Military Homebuyers

- **Get your Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** before you start shopping
- **Compare multiple lenders** — VA loan rates can vary significantly
- **Factor in relocation plans** — PCS orders could affect your housing needs
- **Consider location** — Base proximity matters for your daily commute
