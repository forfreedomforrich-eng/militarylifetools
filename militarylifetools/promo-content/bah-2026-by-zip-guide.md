### How BAH Works in 2026

BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) is a monthly allowance paid to active-duty service members who don't live in government-provided housing on base. It's designed to cover the cost of off-base housing in your local duty station area.

**The 4 Factors That Determine Your BAH:**

1. **Duty Station ZIP Code (MHA)** — The single biggest factor. An E-5 in San Diego gets 3x what an E-5 in rural Missouri gets.
2. **Pay Grade (Rank)** — Officers and senior enlisted receive more. E-1 to E-5 share a tier, E-6 to E-9 another, O-1 to O-3 a higher tier, etc.
3. **Dependency Status** — "With dependents" rates are 10-30% higher than "without dependents" rates.
4. **Whether You Live On or Off Base** — On-base housing forfeits BAH (you get the quarters in-kind).

### BAH Strategy: Live On Base vs. Off Base

**When to Live On Base:**
- High-cost areas (San Diego, DC, Honolulu, NY)
- Junior enlisted (E-1 to E-4)
- Families with kids
- Short tours (1-2 years)

**When to Live Off Base:**
- Mid- to senior-rank (E-6 and above)
- Low-cost areas (Texas, Oklahoma, the South)
- Buying a home with VA loan
- Long tours (3+ years)

**Pro Tip: The "BAH Arbitrage" Strategy** — Many savvy service members rent a home for less than their BAH rate and pocket the difference. Example: An E-6 at Fort Cavazos might receive $2,400/month in BAH but rent a 3-bedroom house for $1,500, putting $900/month into savings or TSP contributions. Over a 4-year tour, that's $43,200 saved.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much is BAH for E-5 with dependents in 2026?**
A: BAH for E-5 with dependents in 2026 ranges from approximately $1,560/month at low-cost duty stations like Fort Leonard Wood to $4,200+/month at high-cost areas like San Diego, Honolulu, and the San Francisco Bay Area. The national average is around $2,400/month.

**Q: When are 2026 BAH rates released?**
A: 2026 BAH rates were released by the DoD on January 1, 2026, and took effect on January 1, 2026.

**Q: Do I get BAH if I live in government housing on base?**
A: No, you do not receive BAH if you live in on-base government quarters. When you live on base, your housing is provided in-kind, so you forfeit the BAH allowance.

**Q: Is BAH taxable in 2026?**
A: No, BAH is tax-free at the federal level, and in most states it's also exempt from state income tax.
