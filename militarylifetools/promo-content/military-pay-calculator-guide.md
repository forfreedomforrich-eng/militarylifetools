### What Makes Up Your Military Pay?

Your total military compensation consists of several components:

**1. Base Pay**
Based on your rank and years of service. The 2026 pay charts were updated last January, and most service members saw a 5.2% increase.

**2. Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH)**
Varies by location, rank, and dependency status. BAH is designed to cover approximately 65% of local housing costs.

**3. Basic Allowance for Subsistence (BAS)**
A fixed allowance to cover meal costs — currently $460.70 per month for 2026.

**4. Special and Incentive Pays**
These include:
- Flight pay (for pilots)
- Hazardous duty pay (IBP)
- Reenlistment bonuses
- Combat pay (tax-free)
