If you're in the military, you know that **PCS moves** are just part of life. Whether you're heading to your first assignment or your fifth, every Permanent Change of Station move comes with its own set of challenges and deadlines.

That's why we created our free **PCS Move Checklist Tool** — to help service members stay organized and never miss a critical deadline.

### What is a PCS Move?

PCS stands for **Permanent Change of Station**. It's a military term for relocating to a new duty station. Unlike civilian moves, military PCS moves involve:
- Government-moving allowances (you don't pay for everything!)
- Weight allowance calculations
- Family travel arrangements
- School transfers for kids

### Why You Need a PCS Timeline Checklist

The biggest mistake I see service members make? **Underestimating how early they need to start preparing.**

Here's what you need to track:
- **90+ days before**: Submit your travel orders request
- **60 days out**: Schedule your MEPS physical (if required)
- **45 days before**: Start your DD-214 verification
- **30 days out**: Schedule house cleaning/packing
- **14 days before**: Submit your travel claim
- **7 days out**: Confirm your travel arrangements

### Pro Tips from a Veteran Mover

- **Start your weight estimate early**: Every service member has a specific weight allowance based on rank and years of service
- **Don't forget your pet transport**: If you're flying, some airlines offer military pet transport programs
- **Update your POA**: Power of Attorney is critical during moves
- **Know your SPA**: State of legal residence affects taxes and benefits

### Common PCS Mistakes to Avoid

1. Waiting too long to schedule packing
2. Not documenting pre-move inventory
3. Forgetting to update your voter registration
4. Missing the deadline for vehicle shipment claims
